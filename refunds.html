Most of what we make is custom-printed to order. Because these items are produced specifically for you, our policy is built around that — while still standing behind our work.

Shipping

Every order is pressed to order by our small team and ships within 5 to 7 business days. We ship within the United States via USPS, and we email you tracking as soon as your order is on its way.

Defects & misprints

If your item arrives with a defect, misprint, or was not made as ordered, we'll make it right. Contact us within 14 days of delivery with a clear photo of the issue and your order details. We will replace the item or issue a refund for the affected pieces.

Custom orders

Because custom-printed goods can't be resold, we do not accept returns or exchanges on correctly fulfilled custom orders (including wrong size selected at checkout or a change of mind). Please double-check sizes and artwork before ordering.

Originals (non-custom items)

Stock "ThreeFold Originals" that are not personalized may be returned unworn, unwashed, and in original condition within 14 days of delivery for a refund of the item price. The buyer pays return shipping unless the return is due to our error.

How refunds are issued

Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method via Stripe. Original shipping charges are non-refundable except where the return is due to our error.

How to start a claim

Email us with your order number and (for defects) photos. We'll respond with next steps.
